**The 2024 TaskOn campaign has ended.**

In August 2024, Fearless Wallet added support for ZChains, an EVM-compatible Layer 1 network. The integration let users view balances and send and receive tokens on ZChains.

The first phase of the TaskOn campaign ran from 14 to 28 August 2024 and offered a prize pool of 500 USDT for 25 winners.
