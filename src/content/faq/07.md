---
question: How do I import my Fearless Wallet?
answer: >-
  Open the import screen and choose your backup: recovery phrase, raw seed or JSON file. If you created the wallet
  with a custom derivation path (including a password, the text after ///) or key type, enter the same settings in
  the advanced settings before you continue. Then check that the addresses match the ones that hold your funds.
---

1. Open the import screen:

   - If you already have a wallet, click your wallet name in the "Wallet" section and select "Add Wallet".
   - If you are new, choose "Import Wallet" on the welcome screen.

2. Choose your backup: recovery phrase, raw seed or JSON file.

   - In the Chrome extension, choose "Ethereum, Polkadot and more" for a Fearless wallet, including its Bitcoin, Solana, TON and Taira accounts. Choose "TON" only for a separate TON wallet with its own phrase.

3. Enter it:

   - **Recovery phrase:** enter the words in the correct order.
   - **Raw seed:** paste the seed. Optionally, add parachain accounts.
   - **JSON file:** upload the encrypted file and enter the password you set for it. If the app asks for the wallet's derivation path, enter the one you used.

4. Before you continue, match how the wallet was created. If you used a custom derivation path, including a password (the text after ///), or a different key type, open the advanced settings ("Advanced backup settings" in the Chrome extension) and enter the same ones. Without them, the same words can open different addresses.

5. Continue and name the wallet. The password that unlocks the app is separate from the derivation path.

6. Check that the restored addresses match the ones that hold your funds.

In the Chrome extension, wallets you import and older wallets the extension flags are limited to recovering your funds: you can back them up and move funds out, but not deposit, swap or start new staking. Follow the extension's notice to move your funds to a new wallet.

Read the article: [Exporting and Importing a Wallet Using a Passphrase](https://wiki.fearlesswallet.io/accounts/walkthrough/exporting-and-importing-a-wallet-using-a-passphrase)
