---
question: How do I import my Fearless Wallet?
answer: To import your wallet, open the Import Menu, choose the import method (Mnemonic Phrase, Raw Seed, or JSON File), and follow the prompts to enter your information.
---

1. Open the Import Menu:

   - If you already have an account, click on your current account name in the "Wallet" section and select "Add Wallet".
   - If you are a new user, click on "Import Wallet" on the welcome screen.

2. Choose the Import Method:

   - Select one of the three options: Mnemonic Phrase, Raw Seed, or JSON File.

**Import Options:**

Mnemonic Phrase:

- Enter each word of the phrase in the correct order.
- If you created the wallet with a derivation path, such as a password (the text after /// in the path), open the advanced settings on the same screen ("Advanced backup settings" in the Chrome extension) and enter that exact path **before** you continue. Without it, the app opens different addresses from the ones that hold your funds.
- Click "Continue," give your account a name, and set a password for unlocking the app. This unlock password is separate from the derivation path.

Raw Seed:

- Paste the raw seed and click "Continue." Optionally, add parachain accounts. Then, name your account and set a new password.

JSON File:

- Select "Restore JSON," upload the encrypted file, and enter the password you set for that file. If the app asks for the wallet's derivation path, enter the one you used. If needed, add EVM accounts.

After these steps, your account appears in the "Wallet" section. Check that its addresses match the ones that hold your funds.

In the Chrome extension, wallets that existed before an update and wallets you import are limited to recovering your funds: you can back them up and move funds out, but not deposit, swap or start new staking. Follow the extension's notice to move your funds to a new wallet.

Read the article: [Exporting and Importing a Wallet Using a Passphrase](https://wiki.fearlesswallet.io/accounts/walkthrough/exporting-and-importing-a-wallet-using-a-passphrase)
