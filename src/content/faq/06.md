If you contributed DOT or KSM directly, check the balance and transaction history of the account you used for the contribution. If you contributed through an exchange or another service, check that service’s records and refund instructions.

Agile Coretime replaced parachain slot auctions. Fearless Wallet has retired crowdloans and parachain staking.
