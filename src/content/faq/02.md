question: Is Fearless wallet non-custodial?
answer: Yes. Fearless Wallet is self-custody, so you hold your keys. Your recovery phrase and private keys stay on your device unless you turn on an optional encrypted backup, such as Google Drive. To show balances and history, the app sends your public addresses to network services. Keep your recovery phrase safe and offline. If you created the wallet with a derivation path, such as a password (the text after /// in the path), you need that exact path as well as the phrase: without it the app opens different addresses from the ones that hold your funds. Keep a JSON backup's password, and its derivation path if you used one. Fearless Wallet cannot recover any of them for you.

Yes. Fearless Wallet is self-custody, so you hold your keys, without trusting a third party.

Your recovery phrase and private keys stay on your device unless you turn on an optional encrypted backup, such as Google Drive. To show balances and history, the app sends your public addresses to network services.

Keep your recovery phrase safe and offline. If you created the wallet with a derivation path, such as a password (the text after /// in the path), you need that exact path as well as the phrase: without it the app opens different addresses from the ones that hold your funds.

Keep a JSON backup's password, and its derivation path if you used one. Fearless Wallet cannot recover any of them for you.
