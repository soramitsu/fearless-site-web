---
question: Is Fearless wallet non-custodial?
answer: Yes. Fearless Wallet is self-custody, so only you control your accounts and funds. Your wallet data stays on your device unless you turn on an optional encrypted backup, such as Google Drive. Keep your recovery phrase, or your JSON file and its password, safe and offline. If your wallet uses a password to create its Bitcoin, Solana, TON or Taira addresses, you need that password as well as the phrase to restore those addresses. Fearless Wallet cannot recover them for you.

Yes. Fearless Wallet is self-custody, so only you control your accounts and funds, without trusting a third party.

Your wallet data stays on your device unless you turn on an optional encrypted backup, such as Google Drive.

Keep your recovery phrase, or your JSON file and its password, safe and offline. If your wallet uses a password to create its Bitcoin, Solana, TON or Taira addresses, you need that password as well as the phrase to restore those addresses. Fearless Wallet cannot recover them for you.
