question: Is Fearless wallet non-custodial?
answer: >-
  Yes. Fearless Wallet is self-custody, so you hold your keys. Your recovery phrase and private keys stay on your
  device unless you turn on an optional encrypted backup, such as Google Drive. To show balances and history, the app
  sends your public addresses to network services. Keep your recovery phrase safe and offline, together with any
  custom derivation path you chose (including a password, the text after ///) and key type, because without them the
  same words can open different addresses. Keep a JSON or Google Drive backup's password, and its path, too. Fearless
  Wallet cannot recover any of them for you.
---

Yes. Fearless Wallet is self-custody, so you hold your keys, without trusting a third party.

Your recovery phrase and private keys stay on your device unless you turn on an optional encrypted backup, such as Google Drive. To show balances and history, the app sends your public addresses to network services.

Keep your recovery phrase safe and offline, together with any custom derivation path you chose (including a password, the text after ///) and key type. Without them, the same words can open different addresses.

Keep a JSON or Google Drive backup's password, and its path, too. Fearless Wallet cannot recover any of them for you.
