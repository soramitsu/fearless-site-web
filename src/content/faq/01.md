Fearless Wallet is an open-source, self-custody wallet.

Your recovery phrase and keys are encrypted on your device. Before it shows or exports them, the app asks for your PIN on mobile (or Face ID or Touch ID on iOS, if you turned it on) or your password in the Chrome extension.

A compromised device can expose keys stored on it. Keep your device's software up to date, and install Fearless Wallet only through the links on our [download page](/download).
