question: How safe is Fearless Wallet?
answer: Fearless Wallet is an open-source, self-custody wallet. Your recovery phrase and keys are encrypted on your device and unlocked with your PIN, password or biometrics, which the app also asks for before sensitive actions such as showing your recovery phrase. No wallet can keep keys safe on a device that is already compromised, so keep your device updated and install Fearless Wallet only from the official stores.
---

Fearless Wallet is an open-source, self-custody wallet.

Your recovery phrase and keys are encrypted on your device and unlocked with your PIN, password or biometrics. The app also asks for them before sensitive actions such as showing your recovery phrase.

No wallet can keep keys safe on a device that is already compromised. Keep your device's software up to date, and install Fearless Wallet only from the [official stores](/download).
