---
question: How do I buy crypto in Fearless Wallet?
answer: In the Wallet tab, select your asset and network, then choose Buy. Pick one of the providers shown. Availability depends on the asset, your platform and your region.
---

In the **Wallet** tab, select the asset and network you want to use. If **Buy** is available, choose it and pick one of the providers shown. Follow that provider’s steps to complete the purchase.

The providers you see depend on the asset, your platform and your region.
